1 · How this app works

You start with ₹5,00,000 of virtual cash. Orders use live prices but no real money is ever involved.

2 · Place a trade

Pick an instrument in Trade, choose BUY or SELL, set the quantity, and press Place practice order.

3 · Manage risk

Always know your stop loss before entering. Risk no more than 1–2% of capital on a single trade.

4 · Review

Open Portfolio to see live P&L, square off positions and review your history.

About the data

Prices, day range, and India VIX are ingested directly from Google Finance via server.js in real-time. PCR, open interest, quantitative analytics, and option Greeks are computed model-based.
